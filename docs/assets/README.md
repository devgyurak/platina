## The mark

A hallmark: the plate punched into precious metal to certify its purity. "Pt" is the chemical
symbol of platinum (*platina* is its old Spanish name); the bar of the `t` grows out of the bowl
of the `P`. The letters are cut out of the plate, so the mark needs one colour only.

| Role | Colour |
|---|---|
| Plate | platinum `#E5E4E2` |
| Background | graphite `#15171B` |

The plate sits inside the central 72% of the square, so GitHub's rounded avatar crop never cuts
its chamfered corners. The icon was checked at 200, 64, 32 and 20 px on GitHub's light and dark
backgrounds; the plate and the letters stay legible at 20 px.

Edit the SVG and re-export the PNGs from it; do not edit the PNGs. No trademark search has been
done.
